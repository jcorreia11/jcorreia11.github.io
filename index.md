## About me

João Correia is an Assistant Professor at the [Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto](https://sigarra.up.pt/feup/en/) and a co-founder of the spin-off [OmniumAI](https://www.omniumai.com/). He holds a Ph.D. in Informatics (2024) and a Master’s degree in Bioinformatics (2018) from the University of Minho, as well as a Bachelor’s degree in Bioengineering (2016) from the University of Trás-os-Montes and Alto Douro. His research focuses on the development of artificial intelligence methods for chemical data analysis.
